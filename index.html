How it works

Three steps, no account and no upload.

  1. 01Pick the subject

    A word or a phrase up to 40 characters, or a PNG, SVG or JPG logo.

  2. 02Tune the look

    Pages or backdrops, fixed rhythm or synced to your music, flash, sound effects. The preview follows every change.

  3. 03Export

    The video records in real time, as MP4 when the browser allows it, otherwise as WebM.

Output formats

Resolutions are set on the short side of the frame, 1080 or 720 pixels.

FAQ

Where are the videos rendered?

In your browser. Pages are generated in code for each cut and nothing is sent to a server, including the logo and music you import.

Why does the export take as long as the video?

Recording captures the render frame by frame together with the sound, so the sound effects stay in sync with the cuts. Keep the tab visible during the export, a background tab slows rendering down.

MP4 or WebM?

Recent Chrome, Edge and Safari record straight to MP4 (H.264). Firefox only records WebM. The chosen format shows in the export window.

Where do the photo backdrops come from?

They are 46 photorealistic scenes generated for the tool, in the street, shops, transport, at home, at the office or worn. On each one, an area is mapped on the surface of an object. Your logo is warped in perspective there and turns black or white depending on how bright the surface is.

How do I sync the cuts to the music?

Import a track in the Sound tab, then choose "Synced to the music" in the Rhythm tab. The tempo is detected automatically and each cut lands on a beat, every two beats or every bar.

Which logo file should I use?

A transparent PNG or an SVG gives the best result. A JPG keeps its rectangular background. Empty margins around the logo are trimmed automatically.

Do the page texts mean anything?

No. They are sentences assembled from a word list, in French or English depending on the visuals language. Only your word is sharp and readable, the rest is texture.

How do I make the exact same video again?

Each video depends on a seed number shown under the preview. Save the preset with a name in the Output tab, it keeps that number and every setting in this browser.
